About Finance Forward Network

Founded in 2025, built by students who saw the gap firsthand.

Most students graduate without ever being taught how credit works, how to budget a paycheck, or how to recognize a scam. Finance Forward Network exists to close that gap, with content designed by students, for students, not repurposed textbook material.

We work at the intersection of two subjects that rarely get taught well together: personal finance and cybersecurity. Both are skills students need before they need them, not after something goes wrong.
